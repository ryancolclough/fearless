Terms of service

Welcome to FearLess Tattoos! Before proceeding with any tattoo procedure at our studio, please carefully review the following terms of service:

Assumption of risks

By receiving a tattoo at FearLess Tattoos, you acknowledge and accept the inherent risks associated with tattooing, including but not limited to infection, scarring, allergic reactions, and difficulties in melanoma detection. You understand that despite the utmost care taken by our artist/s, these risks exist and may vary depending on individual factors.

Waiver and release

You hereby release FearLess Tattoos and its artists from any and all liability for personal injury or damages resulting from the tattoo procedure, whether caused by negligence, inherent risks, or otherwise. This release extends to any claims or causes of action, including direct and consequential damages, that may arise from the procedure and application of your tattoo.

Informed consent

FearLess Tattoos provides ample opportunity for clients to ask questions regarding the tattoo procedure, ensuring that all queries are satisfactorily answered before proceeding. Our artist/s are committed to providing you with detailed information about the process, risks, aftercare, and any other aspects related to your tattoo.

Aftercare instructions

Clients agree to follow the aftercare instructions provided by FearLess Tattoos for proper healing of the tattoo. Any touch-up work required due to negligence on the client’s part will be at their own expense. It is crucial to adhere to these instructions to minimize the risk of infection and promote optimal healing.

Health conditions

Clients confirm that they do not have any medical or skin conditions that may interfere with the tattoo procedure or healing process. Additionally, clients declare that they are not under the influence of alcohol or drugs during the tattooing process, as it may compromise safety and judgment.

Permanent change

Clients understand that a tattoo is a permanent alteration to their appearance and can only be removed through laser or surgical means, which may be costly and may not fully restore the skin’s original appearance. It is essential to consider the permanence of tattoos and choose designs carefully.

Photographic consent

Clients grant FearLess Tattoos permission to take photographs of them and their tattoos, and consent to their reproduction in print or electronic form for promotional and portfolio purposes. However, clients have the option to decline photographic consent by notifying the studio in advance.

No refund policy

Clients acknowledge and agree to FearLess Tattoos’s no refund policy on tattoos, piercings, and retail sales. Once a service or product is rendered, refunds will not be provided, except as required by law.

Attorney’s fees

In the event of legal action against FearLess Tattoos or its artist/s, clients agree to reimburse them for any attorney’s fees and costs incurred, should they prevail. This provision aims to ensure fair compensation for any legal expenses arising from disputes.

Jurisdiction

Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be governed by the courts of Ontario, Canada, with exclusive jurisdiction in Ottawa. By receiving a tattoo at FearLess Tattoos, clients submit to the jurisdiction and venue of these courts for the resolution of any disputes.

Severability

If any provision of this agreement is deemed unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain valid and enforceable to the fullest extent permitted by law.

Legal capacity

By receiving a tattoo at FearLess Tattoos, clients confirm that they are of legal age, competent to sign this agreement, and have provided valid proof of age and identification. This ensures that clients have the legal capacity to enter into a binding contract for tattoo services.

Privacy policy

At FearLess Tattoos, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information:

Information collection

We may collect personal information from clients, including but not limited to full name, address, date of birth, telephone number, email address, and form of identification. This information is collected solely for the purpose of providing tattoo services and communicating with clients regarding appointments, aftercare instructions, and studio updates.

Use of information

Personal information collected is used solely for the purposes stated above and is not shared with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We take measures to ensure that your personal information is securely stored and protected from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Photographic consent

Clients grant FearLess Tattoos permission to take photographs of them and their tattoos, and consent to their reproduction in print or electronic form for promotional and portfolio purposes, unless otherwise indicated. Clients have the option to decline photographic consent by notifying the studio in advance.

Retention of information

We retain client personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Clients have the right to access, correct, or delete their personal information held by FearLess Tattoos. Requests for such actions can be made by contacting us directly.

Third-party disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer client personal information to third parties unless required by law or for the purpose of legal proceedings. We may disclose personal information if necessary to comply with legal obligations, enforce our policies, or protect the rights, property, or safety of ourselves or others.

Changes to policy

FearLess Tattoos reserves the right to update or modify this privacy policy at any time. By receiving a tattoo at FearLess Tattoos, clients acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this privacy policy.
